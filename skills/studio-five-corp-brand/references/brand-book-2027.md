Markdown edition of *Studio Five Corp Branding Guidelines 2027* (102 pages, 1440 × 810): visual identity and brand communication. Page numbers in brackets point to the source PDF.

**Scope.** For Corp teams, designers and suppliers. Apply one consistent identity, voice and production standard.
**Authority.** This book governs new Corp work. Original artwork and written rules take precedence over a scaled example.
**How to use it.** Start with the quick rule, use the example, and open the specifications for exact values and permitted exceptions.

## 01 / Foundation & voice

### The identity at a glance [4]

**Bright. Precise. Full of energy.**

- **Design principle:** White space creates clarity. Cobalt leads recognition. Energy blue supports. Fresh lime highlights one moment.
- **Voice:** Genuine, friendly and energetic. Professional and experiential. Clear enough for business.
- **Visual system:** Clash Display + Satoshi.

### Why the identity evolved [5]

| Aim | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A recognisable entity | The S + 5 + C symbol and uppercase STUDIO FIVE wordmark form one coordinated identity. Use the latest supplied artwork. |
| A brighter experience | White-led layouts give work, ideas and people room. Cobalt blue establishes recognition across every touchpoint. |
| A clear hierarchy | Energy blue supports the story. Fresh lime isolates one priority. Keep the three brand colours from competing. |
| One shared standard | Consistent artwork, Clash Display, Satoshi, spacing and voice connect digital, print and physical experiences. |

### What Studio Five Corp does [6]

- **A creative operating company.** Corp serves corporate clients through event organisation, media, production, photography and marketing.
- **Our work.** Show relevant work: events, video, photography, highlights, live streaming and digital marketing.
- **Our approach.** Bring people and brands together through thoughtful creative work, reliable production and clear client communication.

### Purpose, approach & delivery [7]

- **Why:** We believe creative businesses and people thrive, to achieve creative excellence, when they have freedom to focus and express.
- **How:** Combine clear systems, thoughtful planning and passionate craft so the team can focus on the quality of each creative experience.
- **What:** Event organisation, media, production, marketing and related creative services that support brand building and growth.

Lead with purpose. Explain the approach. Make the deliverable clear.

### Naming & website endorsement [8]

- **Use the complete name.** Write *Studio Five Corp* in formal copy. Use the supplied uppercase artwork for visual identity.
- **No recurring slogan.** The logo is complete alone. Use project-specific headlines; never attach a recurring slogan or tagline.
- **Website footer only.** Left: Corp logo. Middle: navigation and contact. Right: *part of* + Group logo, linked to studiofive.group.

**Shared desktop footer (three zones):**

```
[Corp horizontal logo]                EXPLORE          GET IN TOUCH          part of
Events. Media. Production. Marketing. Our work         Discuss a project     [Group logo]
Email the Corp team                   Services         Contact
                                      About            Email us
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
© 2026 Studio Five Corp Pte Ltd. All rights reserved.   studiofivecorp.com   Terms & conditions
```

- **Balanced endorsement:** left-align *part of* with the Group artwork. Inter Regular 13 px / 1.2 leading, 6 px gap.
- **Desktop to mobile:** Corp horizontal at least 240 px; Group 136 px. Mobile stacks entity, links, endorsement and legal; side padding 24 px.

### Our five voice traits [9]

| Trait | Do | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Genuine | Use real work and specific, supportable details. | Unsupported claims or invented certainty. |
| Friendly | Address people directly and give a clear next step. | Stiff language or forced familiarity. |
| Energetic | Use active verbs and purposeful, concise sentences. | Constant capitals or exaggerated promises. |
| Professional | Be precise about scope, timing and responsibilities. | Vague commitments or unchecked details. |
| Experiential | Describe what people will see, hear, feel or do. | Abstract promises without a tangible experience. |

Use all five traits together. Context changes intensity; accuracy and clarity remain consistent. **Do not use emojis or trend-led exceptions.**

### Make the next action clear [10]

| Context | Avoid | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project invitation | Your ultimate one-stop solution for everything! | Tell us about your event and the outcome you need. | Names the subject and a useful next step. |
| Project update | Kindly revert at your earliest convenience. | The first edit is ready. Send feedback by Friday so we can prepare the final version. | Makes the action and timing clear. |
| Delivery change | Due to unforeseen circumstances, delivery is delayed. | We need one more day for final checks. We will send the revised version tomorrow at 3 pm. | States the change and a specific commitment. |

Use sentence case and full company names. State SGD when needed. Check names, facts and deadlines. Functional headlines are not recurring slogans.

## 02 / Logo system

### The Corp primary logo [11]

- **Primary / stacked:** two-blue symbol, uppercase STUDIO FIVE, and a lighter, widely spaced CORP line. Use the supplied lockup intact.
- **Original symbol:** use in compact contexts where the company is already named. Export the symbol unchanged from the current source; never redraw it.

### The approved logo family [12]

| # | Version |
|---|---|
| 01 | Primary / colour |
| 02 | Primary / white |
| 03 | Horizontal / colour |
| 04 | Horizontal / white |
| 05 | Symbol / colour |
| 06 | Symbol / white |

Use the four supplied primary/horizontal masters. Compact symbols are unchanged extracts from those masters.

### The right logo for the background [13]

| Background | Logo |
|---|---|
| White / light | Two-blue artwork on a clean, light field. |
| Cobalt | Complete White monochrome artwork. |
| Black / dark | White monochrome is mandatory; never use the two-blue logo. |

### Construction [14–15]

- Inner and outer curve radii define the negative-space turns and the rounded outer edges.
- Guides connect the symbol, uppercase STUDIO FIVE and the spaced CORP line. Preserve every supplied gap.
- Horizontal: keep the supplied symbol-to-wordmark gap, uppercase lettering and CORP spacing. Never alter the curves.
- Construction artwork explains the geometry; it is **not** an instruction to redraw the logo and not a production logo.

### Clear space, minimum sizes & icons [16]

- **Clear space:** X = one quarter of the visible symbol height. Keep at least X around the complete lockup, clear of text and trims.
- **Minimum sizes:** Primary 160 px / 30 mm wide. Horizontal 240 px / 45 mm wide. Symbol 24 px / 6 mm high.
- **Check the lighter CORP line:** proof the current artwork at final size. Enlarge for embroidery or rough stock. Inspect 16 px symbol favicons individually.
- **Avatars:** centre the visible symbol, not the file canvas. Use 60–65 % of a square avatar by default; preserve clear space and preview the actual crop.

### Logo misuse [17–18]

Never: stretch, rotate, crop, rearrange, retype, change proportions, add gradients, recolour, outline, add hard or multiple shadows, place the two-blue logo on dark, or leave an unresolved busy crop. Never recreate the logo with live text.

### Photography, shadows & separation [19]

- **Choose the background first.** Find a quiet crop and use the clearest approved colourway. White artwork is usually strongest over photography.
- **One restrained exception.** A subtle Black shadow may support a logo over a photo or video: broad, soft falloff with enough opacity to separate every letter.
- **Keep every letter clear.** No coloured glow, hard outline, bevel or multiple shadows. Reposition when a shadow cannot restore visibility.

### S + 5 + C. One connected identity. [20]

The symbol combines S, 5 and C (Studio Five Corp); the letters and number interlock into one mark. Continuous curves give motion and momentum; measured geometry brings precision. Cobalt anchors the identity; Energy blue adds a second layer of movement.

## 03 / Colour system

### Body text follows the background [21]

| Background | Text rules |
|---|---|
| White | Black body text. Cobalt carries headings, links and primary actions. |
| Cobalt | White body text. Use the White logo. Keep detailed reading clear. |
| Black | White body text. Use the White logo. Never place two-blue artwork here. |
| Energy blue field | Black normal text. Cobalt text is large only (the two-blue pair is 4.39:1). |
| Fresh lime field | Black body text. Cobalt headings only. Never use White on this field. |

Cobalt/White remains the first-choice brand pair. A passing ratio alone is not a colour role.

### The five-colour palette [22]

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK % | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cobalt blue | `#0046AE` | 0 / 70 / 174 | 100 / 77 / 0 / 0 | Primary: entity recognition |
| Energy blue | `#4CC9F0` | 76 / 201 / 240 | 62 / 0 / 4 / 0 | Secondary: supporting information |
| Fresh lime | `#D4F725` | 212 / 247 / 37 | 24 / 0 / 98 / 0 | Accent: focused emphasis |
| Black | `#000000` | 0 / 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 0 / 100 | Support: body text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 / 255 / 255 | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 | Support: primary surface |

CMYK reference: sRGB to PSO Coated v3 (FOGRA51), relative colorimetric, rounded to whole %. Final output follows the printer profile and approved proof.

### The proportion system [23, 32]

- **Default website & deck target:** 70 % White / 21 % Cobalt / 6 % Energy blue / 3 % Fresh lime. Assess visible flat fields; exclude photography.
- **Within coloured elements:** 70 % Cobalt / 20 % Energy blue / 10 % Fresh lime (calculated within those three only; exclude White and Black).
- It is a **default target for white-led work, not a per-page quota**. Cobalt covers and dividers are permitted. Black has no fixed share.

**Measuring the surface area:** count visible background and graphic flat fields, including unfilled white space; exclude photographic image areas; count overlapping fields only where visible. Read the field beneath text, logos and icons rather than measuring each mark. Example: 10,000 units after excluding photos → 7,000 White / 2,100 Cobalt / 600 Energy / 300 Lime. Review broad balance across ordinary pages; do not force exact percentages.

### Colour behaviour by context [24]

| Context | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Website | 60–80 % white flat-field area across a typical content screen. Cobalt drives key headings, brand anchors and primary actions. |
| Presentations | Most content slides white. Cobalt for main narrative anchors and occasional section dividers. Energy blue supports; lime marks one priority. |
| Campaigns & social | Cobalt-dominant campaign moments are permitted. Keep the main logo identifiable; avoid equal blocks of all three brand colours. |
| Team documents | Use the Corp system. Prioritise readable information; use colour to organise, not decorate every element. |

### Readable colour combinations [25] and contrast limits [30]

| Pair | Ratio | Permission |
|---|---|---|
| Cobalt / White | 8.44:1 | Normal text, large text, essential graphics (first choice) |
| Black / White | 21.00:1 | Normal text, large text, essential graphics |
| Black / Energy blue | 10.92:1 | Normal text, large text, essential graphics |
| Black / Fresh lime | 17.13:1 | Normal text, large text, essential graphics |
| Cobalt on Fresh lime | 6.89:1 | **Headings only** |
| Cobalt / Energy blue | 4.39:1 | Large text / essential graphics only |
| Cobalt / Black | 2.49:1 | Decoration only |
| Energy blue / White | 1.92:1 | Decoration only |
| Energy blue / Fresh lime | 1.57:1 | Decoration only |
| Fresh lime / White | 1.23:1 | Decoration only |

WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 & 1.4.11: text 4.5:1; large text and essential graphics 3:1. Large = 24 CSS px regular / 18.67 CSS px bold (18 pt / 14 pt bold) at final size. Threshold decisions use unrounded values. Never use white text on Energy blue or Fresh lime.

### See the colour roles in context [26]

Annotated web hero: (1) original two-blue logo on White; (2) Cobalt brand headline as the first reading level; (3) Black reading text on White; (4) White label on a Cobalt button. Energy blue and Fresh lime are supporting options, not compulsory decoration.

### Digital colour & print production [27]

- **Digital:** exact HEX/RGB in sRGB. Never sample colours from a screenshot, photo or preview.
- **CMYK:** the FOGRA51 values are a coated-paper starting point. A different profile, paper or process needs a new conversion and proof.
- **Cobalt ink trial:** C97 M82 Y0 K0 is a test recipe only, not the universal Cobalt specification. Approve the actual printed sample.
- **Dark fabric & white:** black shirts use the white monochrome logo; specify opaque white ink or thread (0/0/0/0 means paper white, not white garment ink).
- Foil, emboss, deboss and engraving use monochrome artwork. Small black text: 100K. Rich black: supplier-approved recipe. Confirm substrate, finish, ICC profile and proof (ECI profiles: eci.org/en/downloads).

### Assign every colour a job [28]

- **Cobalt `#0046AE`:** brand headings, primary actions, identity anchors. Pair with White first. All-white logo on Cobalt surfaces.
- **Energy blue `#4CC9F0`:** secondary fields and supporting graphics. Black for normal text on it. On White, outline essential Energy blue marks in Cobalt.
- **Fresh lime `#D4F725`:** one selected accent. Cobalt headings only and Black body text on lime. Never lime text on white; never use lime to recolour the logo.
- **Black `#000000`:** body copy, data labels and detailed information on white. Keep black surfaces deliberate; white logo on black.
- **White `#FFFFFF`:** default reading surface and negative space. White text on Cobalt or Black only.

### Decoration is not information [29]

Fresh lime and Energy blue shapes may decorate White only when they carry no information or function: the message must survive their removal. Text, data, essential icons and controls must stay readable; use a compliant pair and add a Cobalt boundary where needed. Ask: does the viewer need this shape to understand or act? If yes, it is meaningful. Never call a data point decorative to bypass contrast (WCAG 1.4.1 / 1.4.11).

### Colour do's & don'ts [31]

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use exact HEX/RGB values and solid palette fills. | Sample previews or create new tints, gradients or blend colours. |
| Use Cobalt on White or White on Cobalt first. | Use Energy blue or Fresh lime text on white. |
| Reserve the two-blue pair for large text or essential graphics. | Use the 4.39:1 blue pair for normal-size text. |
| Use lime on white only as an optional decorative accent. | Make low-contrast lime the only essential cue. |
| Label data; use visible boundaries or distinct line styles. | Rely on hue alone for meaning, status or actions. |
| Use the supplied white logo on Cobalt, Black or dark fields. | Place the two-blue logo on a dark background. |

## 04 / Typography

### Two typefaces. Clear jobs. [33]

| Role | Typeface | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines | **Clash Display** | Headlines and expressive project copy. |
| Information | **Satoshi** | Paragraphs, instructions and practical detail. |
| Labels & actions | **Satoshi Medium** | Navigation, buttons and short functional labels. |

- Headline tracking: large −2 %, smaller −1 %. Inspect letter pairs.
- Body tracking: 0. Never stretch text to fill a line.
- Uppercase labels: +6 %. Keep labels short and distinct.
- Main headings Bold; secondary headings Semibold.

### Specimens [34]

Clash Display: Medium, Semibold, Bold. Satoshi: Regular, Medium, Bold. Use real weights and the supplied font files. Official font source: **Fontshare**. The logo remains original outlined artwork. The shared Group endorsement label uses **Inter Regular** as a separate cross-entity component.

### Sizes, weights & line heights [35]

| Format | Display / H1 / H2 | Body / caption |
|---|---|---|
| Website desktop | 64–80 / 48–56 / 32–40 px | 18 px / 14 px |
| Website mobile | 40–48 / 32–36 / 24–28 px | 16–18 px / 13–14 px |
| Deck 1440 × 810 | 96–112 / 54–60 / 32–36 units | 24–28 / 18–20 units |
| A4 documents | 36–44 / 24–28 / 16–20 pt | 10.5–12 pt / 8.5–9.5 pt |

- **Weight:** Display and H1 700. H2 600. Minor headings 500–600. Body 400. Labels Satoshi 500–700.
- **Line height:** Clash headings 100–110 %. Satoshi body 140–150 %; captions 130–140 %.

### Hierarchy at work [36]

Desktop specimen: headline Clash Display Bold 56/56 px, −2 %; body Satoshi Regular 18/27 px, 0 tracking; action Satoshi Medium 18 px, Cobalt on White, underline inline text links. Check a specification at actual output size.

### Tracking, kerning & spacing [37]

- Headlines: −0.02em on large headings by default, −0.01em on smaller headings. Optical or metrics kerning; inspect awkward pairs manually.
- Body & labels: Satoshi body 0em; uppercase labels +0.06em. Never track long paragraphs or stretch type.
- **Spacing rhythm:** 8-unit base: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Heading-to-body 24–32 units in decks; 16–24 px on web.
- Confirm spacing after any export or font substitution.

### Editorial & font handling [38]

- **Typesetting:** left-align paragraphs; start with 45–75 characters per line; keep headings concise; avoid widows, isolated words and justified text.
- **Emphasis:** Black body on White; Cobalt headings or links; underline inline links; keep emphasis purposeful.
- **Font availability:** use supplied Clash Display and Satoshi with real weights; embed fonts and inspect the export.
- **Logo lettering:** uppercase STUDIO FIVE and spaced CORP are outlined artwork. Never reconstruct the logo with a font.
- For unsupported characters use an appropriate companion font and review with a fluent reader.

## 05 / Layout, imagery & video

### Grid, margins & safe zones [39]

- **Presentation master:** 1440 × 810 units. Outer margin 64. Footer boundary y = 752. Base rhythm 8. Minimum 48-unit gutter.
- **Web grid:** desktop 12 columns, 24–32 px gutters, 64–80 px side padding. Mobile 4 columns, 16–24 px side padding.
- **Other formats:** 5 % outer safe zone by default. Check print bleed and platform overlays separately. Logo clear space applies everywhere.

### Composition & hierarchy [40]

- Keep labels with their explanations. Leave at least 40 units between independent text groups in the deck system.
- Choose an image-led, information-led or statement-led layout. Keep one dominant focal point.
- Edit or redistribute copy when it grows; keep equal row padding and group rhythm; never crowd the next heading.

### Keep headings with their explanations [41]

Avoid a small gap between different topics. Example relationship: **24 within / 48 between** groups, using the spacing scale. Longer copy moves the next group down. Protect the gaps; reflow or split the page when content grows. Preserve the relationship rather than treating the numbers as universal.

### Choose the layout for the message [42]

| Layout | Use when |
|---|---|
| Image-led | The work or human experience carries the message. |
| Information-led | Detail, specifications, scope and practical instructions. |
| Statement-led | One clear decision or a transition in the story. |

All three use one underlying grid; typography, logo integrity, colour roles and group spacing stay stable.

### Photography [43–44]

- **Real people, real moments.** Genuine Corp work and participation. Expressions, relationships and gestures lead; raw means believable, not unfinished.
- **Emotion and energy.** Focus, anticipation, connection or joy. Movement without staged enthusiasm.
- **Subtle blue foreshadowing.** A real blue object can recall the brand. Preserve skin tones and context; never flood or recolour the scene.
- **Select with purpose.** Actual work for the particular story. Preserve faces, important details, client intent and creator credits.
- **When colour competes.** Choose another image if a dominant treatment fights the page.
- **Retouch with restraint.** Natural skin, texture and setting. No misleading stock or invented context. Confirm usage rights; distinguish concepts.

### Photography, type & functional icons [45]

- Build with approved ingredients: original logos, Corp typography, the five-colour system and relevant imagery.
- **Functional icons:** one outline family, 24 px with 1.5–2 px strokes as a starting point. Label unfamiliar icons and check contrast.
- **Motif specifications** are issued separately. Do not invent recurring patterns or symbol-derived decoration. No new motif is adopted in this release. Functional rules, grid guides and chart boundaries are not motifs.

### Video [46–47]

- People lead the story: real conversations, reactions and participation; let sound, emotion and action convey the experience.
- Preserve skin tones, colour and location sound; include subtle real blue details; avoid forced reactions and heavy grades.
- Caption essential speech and allow reading time. Reveal the original logo intact; preview recognition at actual export size.
- **End frame (16:9, logo alone):** on a Cobalt field use the White horizontal artwork; on a White field use the two-blue horizontal artwork. No slogan. No universal outro duration: hold long enough for recognition.
- Keep text clear of interface overlays. Use the restrained photo/video shadow only where permitted.

## 06A / Website & presentations

### A bright, white-led website [48]

- Cobalt carries the logo, main headings and primary action. White keeps information open and readable.
- Useful content: show relevant work, explain services and process, use specific navigation and action labels.
- **Shared footer structure:** entity logo left, links middle, *part of* + Group logo right. Mobile stacks entity, links, endorsement and legal. Group endorsement appears **only** in the website footer (see §01 Naming).

### Responsive design & accessibility [49]

- **Reading:** Black paragraphs on White; White on Cobalt or Black. Mobile body 16–18 px. Comfortable line lengths.
- **Actions & focus:** specific labels; White on Cobalt buttons. On White, a Cobalt focus ring with a White gap; on Cobalt, White with a dark gap.
- **Interaction:** underline inline links; pair status with words or icons; essential controls need visible boundaries.
- **Responsive identity:** horizontal lockup in wide headers; symbol only where the company name is visible; preserve clear space; preview mobile crops.
- Check keyboard access, accessible names, image descriptions, touch targets, reduced motion and the final foreground/background pair.

### Article covers: blue lives in the scene [50]

1. **Default article card:** photograph above the heading and summary. Upload a clean image; keep words as editable website text.
2. **Blue within the scene:** before upload, recolour a selected object if needed. Keep blue noticeable with natural material, light and texture.
3. **Creative flexibility:** other readable layouts may overlay live text, kept separate from the image file.

Use only owned or approved Corp assets; no stock. Preserve skin tones and client artwork. Selective colour edits are illustrative treatments, not documentary evidence.

### Personal proposals & tenders [51]

- **Personal / direct proposal:** lead with Studio Five Corp. Corp colours, approved logo, Clash Display headings, Satoshi body and Corp tone. Cover and content white-led. Present idea, scope, deliverables, timing and fees clearly.
- **Tender / procurement:** client branding may be used **only with the entity leader's approval**. The leader confirms whether to use a prescribed tender template or the Corp system. Follow mandatory submission requirements; keep authorship and commercial information clear; don't assume client branding is required.

### Presentation layouts [52, 54]

- **Cover:** original logo, project title and one context line (`[Client] / [Project] / [Date]`). White-led; Cobalt carries the main identity.
- **Content:** one clear message with useful evidence; heading, text and image on one grid.
- **Pace:** Cobalt dividers when useful; Fresh lime to one accent; no repeated filler imagery.

| Slide type | Job |
|---|---|
| Cover | Identify the project and audience. |
| Statement / divider | Make one transition or main point. |
| Text + evidence | Explain a point and show the proof. |
| Comparison | Compare the same criteria fairly. |
| Process | Show a sequence, roles or stages (e.g. 01 Understand / 02 Create / 03 Deliver). |
| Scope / table | Make deliverables, quantities and timing explicit. |

### Build the story before the slides [53]

Define audience, purpose and decision required; outline before layout or imagery. Every direct proposal explains objective, idea, scope, timing, fees and next action. For tenders, map every required response to a slide or appendix and confirm the branding route with the entity leader.

**Default direct-proposal sequence:** 01 Context & objective → 02 Audience & success criteria → 03 Idea & experience plan → 04 Scope & deliverables → 05 Method, timing & evidence → 06 Fees, assumptions & next action. Reorder or combine when needed; never omit essential commercial or delivery information.

### Keep the deck grid fixed [55]

1440 × 810 units; side margins 64; align section label and symbol; fixed page controls. Title at **x64 / y117**. Body starts at **y224** or below and ends by **y712**. Footer boundary **y752** kept clear. Two **632-unit** columns with a **48-unit** gutter. At least 40 units between text groups. Clash H1 54–60 / H2 32–36 units; Satoshi body 24–28 / captions 18–20. 8-unit rhythm.

### Write one clear point per slide [56]

State the takeaway in the headline; a second title line moves content down. Start with 3–5 short points; production body type ≥ 24 units; move background detail to notes. Resolve overflow by editing, splitting or changing the layout, never by shrinking type or packing the footer. Don't give unrelated detail equal emphasis on one slide.

### Make evidence easy to assess [57]

Genuine, relevant work; faces and details visible; one strong crop. State finding, units, dates, source and status (measured, estimate, target, example). Never invent performance or testimonials. Cobalt or black chart lines on white; outline Energy blue bars in Cobalt; give lime marks a visible boundary or label. Required evidence fields: finding / unit / date or period / source; status; permission (image, client name, testimonial). Flag missing sources; replace placeholders before release.

### State scope, timing and fees precisely [58]

Name each output, quantity, format, owner and deadline; client inputs, revision limits and exclusions; separate optional work. State confirmed dates, approval stages and dependencies; never promise an assumed date. State currency, fees, inclusions, exclusions, tax treatment and validity; confirm with the entity leader. Content model: Deliverable / Quantity / Owner & timing / Dependencies / Fee & currency / Next action. Never release bracketed placeholders or borrow another project's commercial terms.

### Control colour and presentation rhythm [59]

Most content slides White, Cobalt headings, Black body. Cobalt for covers, dividers and the ending, with the White logo. Energy blue supports; lime marks one priority. Don't apply 70/21/6/3 to every slide. **Final slide:** Cobalt background, White text, white monochrome logo (e.g. "Let's make it happen." + agreed next action). **Group endorsement never belongs in a deck.**

### Check the exported deck before release [60]

1. **Confirm** audience, decision, branding route and tender requirements.
2. **Verify** names, scope, fees, dates, sources and permissions; remove placeholders and internal comments.
3. **Inspect** alignment, equal row spacing, logo clear space, final-size text and contrast; fix overflow without shrinking type.
4. **Export** with embedded fonts, vector logos and selectable text; open and inspect every page, image, link and page number.
5. **Release** after content and commercial review; save the final with sources and a version record.

## 06B / Other applications & thumbnails

### Data, evidence & reports [61]

Lead with the finding; state units, periods, sources and definitions; separate measured results from targets. Charts: Cobalt for the main series; outline Energy blue on White; labels and distinct line styles; never low-contrast lime alone. Tables in Satoshi; right-align comparable numbers and align decimals; avoid dense decorative fills. Verify results, testimonial permission and attribution before publishing.

### Documents & business cards [62]

- **Letterhead & documents:** white, black body text, Cobalt titles, full Corp logo; repeat header placement and contact structure.
- **Business cards:** Cobalt identity face and white contact face; centre the logo; prioritise name, role and live contact details.
- **Print production:** confirm trim, bleed, stock and finish; vector logos; proof small text and colour before the full run.

### Posters & campaign graphics [63]

One clear headline and one hero image; details in a separate readable area. Set hierarchy for the viewing distance. Approved details and photos; preserve trim, bleed and clear space; proof the physical output.

### Email governance [64]

- Use the Corp email account for Corp clients. Generic inbox: **hey@studiofivecorp.com**.
- Use the approved signature, full company name and verified contact details; consistent logo size and information structure.
- Use approved name, role and legal company details; confirm phone, address and registration details before publication.
- Check account logins across services; raise access issues immediately; replace retired signatures.

### Generic & staff signatures [65]

**Fixed information structure.** Left: current **horizontal** logo and UEN. Right: sender, legal company name and verified contact fields.


### Review the export. Make a decision. [101]

**Compliance:** core checks complete; format checks complete; export verified (clipping, wrapping, missing assets, image quality).
**Design quality:** message understood quickly; hierarchy and balance work; works at the actual viewing size.

**Verdicts:** **READY**: checks passed. **REVISE**: rule broken or message unclear. **CLARIFY**: no approved rule fits.

The maker verifies rules; the reviewer assesses communication quality. Escalate unresolved exceptions to the brand owner; never silently create a new rule.
